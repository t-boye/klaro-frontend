RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT
Landlord: Chibuike Okonkwo Properties Ltd ("the Landlord")
Tenant: [Tenant Full Name] ("the Tenant")
Property: Flat 4B, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria

Tenancy Period: 12 months commencing 1st August 2026
Annual Rent: NGN 1,800,000 (One Million Eight Hundred Thousand Naira)

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. PAYMENT OF RENT
The full annual rent of NGN 1,800,000 shall be paid in advance before the tenancy commences. The Landlord additionally requires a non-refundable "Agency Fee" of NGN 180,000 and a "Caution Deposit" of NGN 360,000. The caution deposit may be forfeited at the Landlord's discretion at the end of the tenancy.

2. RENEWAL
This tenancy shall not automatically renew. The Tenant must notify the Landlord in writing 3 months before the expiry date of their intention to renew. Failure to do so shall be deemed notice to vacate. Rent for renewal shall be set by the Landlord at whatever rate the Landlord determines, with no cap on increases.

3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE TENANT
a) The Tenant shall not make any alterations, repairs, or improvements to the property without written consent.
b) The Tenant shall be responsible for all maintenance and repairs to the property, including structural repairs, plumbing, and electrical faults, regardless of cause.
c) The Tenant shall pay all utility bills including electricity, water, and generator fuel levy.
d) The Tenant shall not sublet any part of the property.
e) The Tenant shall allow the Landlord or the Landlord's agents to enter the property at any time without prior notice.

4. TERMINATION BY LANDLORD
The Landlord may terminate this tenancy and require the Tenant to vacate within 7 days for any breach of these terms, as determined solely by the Landlord. The Tenant waives any right to contest such determination.

5. EARLY TERMINATION BY TENANT
If the Tenant wishes to vacate before the end of the tenancy period, the Tenant forfeits all rent paid for the remaining period. No refund shall be made under any circumstances.

6. DEPOSIT REFUND
The caution deposit shall be refunded within 90 days of vacation at the Landlord's discretion, subject to deductions for any damage, cleaning, repainting, or other works as assessed by the Landlord. The Landlord's assessment of deductions is final and not subject to dispute.

7. LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Any dispute arising from this agreement shall be resolved through private mediation only. The Tenant expressly waives the right to report any dispute to the Lagos State Tenancy Tribunal or any other regulatory body.

8. QUIET ENJOYMENT
The Landlord shall not be responsible for disturbance caused by construction, noise, or other tenants in the building.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and the Lagos State Tenancy Law 2011, except where those laws conflict with the terms of this agreement, in which case the terms of this agreement shall prevail.
